It is based on the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Visiting the website

When you visit the website, our hosting provider GitHub Inc. (USA, service GitHub Pages) automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, date and time, pages visited and browser type (server logs). This data is needed to run the website securely; GitHub stores it for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not use cookies, tracking or analytics tools. Fonts and scripts are loaded from our own server. Your language choice is stored only in your browser (local storage).

Contact form

When you use the Idea Lab form, we process your name, email address, company (optional) and message in order to answer your request and to prepare a possible collaboration (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). Providing this data is voluntary, but without it we cannot reply.

Recipients and transfers abroad

The website is hosted by GitHub Inc. (USA). The form is processed by Formspree Inc. (USA), which forwards your message to us by email. Emails sent to us are received and stored by our email provider Zoho Corporation B.V. (data centre in the EU). Your data is therefore processed in the USA. Where a country does not provide adequate data protection, we ensure protection through recognised safeguards, such as the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. We do not sell your data or pass it on for advertising.

Retention

We keep your messages for as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting collaboration, or as required by statutory retention obligations. The data is then deleted.

Your rights

You can request information about your data and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. You can object to processing and request a copy of your data. Just write to office@stecstudio.ai. You may also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, www.edoeb.admin.ch) or, if you live in the EU, your local data protection authority.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy. The version published on this website applies.
