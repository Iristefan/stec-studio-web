KKS Decoder – Privacy policy

Controller

StecStudio, Stefan Constantinescu, Avenue de la Gare 6, 1618 Châtel-St-Denis, Switzerland Email: office@stecstudio.ai This privacy policy applies to the Android app KKS Decoder (the “app”), published on Google Play by StecStudio. It is based on the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Account and sign-in

To use the app, you sign in with your Google account. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. We receive your name, email address, profile picture (if available) and a unique user ID. We use this data to create and manage your account and to link your purchases to it (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Where your data is stored

Your account data (name, email address, profile picture, user ID) is stored by Firebase Authentication on Google servers, which may be located in the USA or in other countries. The KKS database and the codes you decode are stored and processed locally on your device.

Camera and photos

The app asks for access to the camera and to your photos only when you choose to scan a label or select an image. Images are analysed exclusively on your device to recognise KKS codes; they are not uploaded and we do not store them. Text recognition uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your device; ML Kit may send anonymous technical information about its performance and usage to Google. You can withdraw these permissions at any time in your Android settings.

AI features (Google Gemini)

Some features use Google’s Gemini AI model. Photos never leave your device. When you use an AI feature, the text you enter or that was recognised (for example a KKS code or a question) is sent to Google to generate the answer. Please do not enter personal or confidential information in AI requests.

App security

To protect the app and our services against misuse, we use Firebase App Check with Google reCAPTCHA. It checks that requests come from the genuine app and not from automated programs; for this, Google processes technical information about your device and how the app is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Payments

Purchases are made exclusively through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google under its own privacy policy; we never receive your card or bank details. We only receive the information needed to unlock your purchase, such as the product, date, amount and an order number. We keep purchase records for as long as Swiss accounting law requires (up to 10 years).

Recipients and transfers abroad

We use the following service providers: Google (Firebase, Gemini, Google Play). Google may process data in the USA and in other countries. Where a country does not provide adequate data protection, we ensure protection through recognised safeguards, such as the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell your data or pass it on for advertising.

Retention and account deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time directly in the app, or by writing to office@stecstudio.ai from the email address of your account (instructions: stecstudio.ai/apps/kks-decoder/delete-account/). Your account and the related data are then deleted within 30 days, except purchase records that we must keep by law. Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is intended for professional users and is not directed at children under 16.

Your rights

You can request information about your data and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. You can object to processing and request a copy of your data. Just write to office@stecstudio.ai. You may also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, www.edoeb.admin.ch) or, if you live in the EU, your local data protection authority.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy. The version published on this page applies; we will inform you about important changes in the app.
