Montessori Assistant – Privacy policy

Controller

StecStudio, Stefan Constantinescu, Avenue de la Gare 6, 1618 Châtel-St-Denis, Switzerland Email: office@stecstudio.ai This privacy policy applies to the web application Montessori Assistant (the “application”), which StecStudio provides to Montessori schools. Teachers and assistants use it for classroom observations, planning presentations, following behaviour and writing reports. It is based on the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Roles: the school and StecStudio

The school decides which data about its children is recorded in the application and is responsible for it (controller). StecStudio processes this data only on behalf of the school and according to its instructions (processor, Art. 9 FADP, Art. 28 GDPR), based on a data processing agreement. For the accounts of teachers and assistants, StecStudio is the controller. Parents with questions about the data of their child should contact the school first; we support the school in answering them.

Who uses the application

The application is used only by teachers and assistants of the school. Children and parents do not have access to it. Each user sees only the data of their own school.

User accounts

You sign in either with your Google account or with a user name and password created by StecStudio. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. With a Google account we receive your name, email address, profile picture (if available) and a unique user ID. We use this data to give you access to the application (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Data about children

Depending on how the school uses the application, it may contain: the child’s name or initials, community or age group, observation notes, presentations given and planned, notes on behaviour and the peace table, transcriptions of observations and the reports created from them. Observation sheets may also contain the names of visitors or parents who observed a class. This data is used only for the pedagogical work of the school – never for advertising or any other purpose, and it is never sold. We recommend that schools record only what is needed for their pedagogical work.

Voice recordings

A recording starts only when a teacher or assistant starts it. The audio is sent to Google’s Gemini AI model to be transcribed; only the resulting text is saved. The audio files are not stored. The school is responsible for informing parents about recordings and, where required, obtaining their consent.

AI features (Google Gemini)

The pedagogical assistant and the transcriptions use Google’s Gemini AI model. For this, the text of the request and the relevant observations, or the audio to be transcribed, are sent to Google. Under the terms of the paid Gemini API, Google does not use this data to train its AI models. AI suggestions support the work of the teachers; they do not replace their professional judgement.

Storage and security

The application is hosted on Google Firebase (Google Cloud). Its database – with the data about children – is stored in a Google Cloud data centre in Belgium (European Union, region europe-west1). Sign-in data (Firebase Authentication) and AI processing (Gemini) may take place in other countries, including the USA. Data is encrypted during transmission and in storage. Only signed-in users of the school have access to the school’s data.

Cookies and tracking

The application stores in your browser only what is needed to keep you signed in. We use no cookies or tools for tracking, analytics or advertising.

Recipients and transfers abroad

We use the following service provider: Google (Firebase for hosting, sign-in and data storage; Gemini for AI features and transcriptions). Google may process data in the USA and in other countries. Where a country does not provide adequate data protection, we ensure protection through recognised safeguards, such as the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. We do not sell data or pass it on for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data about children is kept for as long as the school uses it in the application. The school can delete records at any time. When the school stops using the application, or at its request to office@stecstudio.ai, we delete the school’s data and the user accounts within 30 days. Reports downloaded as PDF files are the responsibility of the school.

Your rights

You can request information about your data and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. You can object to processing and request a copy of your data. Just write to office@stecstudio.ai. You may also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, www.edoeb.admin.ch) or, if you live in the EU, your local data protection authority.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy. The version published on this page applies; we inform schools about important changes.
