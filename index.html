FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. Once we understand your needs, we send you a clear estimate before any work begins.

How long does it take?

Timelines depend on complexity. We start with a small prototype, so you see results early.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. We handle the technical part and explain everything in plain language.

What happens with my data?

Your data is used only for your project. We choose GDPR-compliant solutions that, where needed, can run on your own infrastructure.

Can you integrate with the tools we already use?

In most cases, yes — email, CRM, spreadsheets, online stores or internal databases.
